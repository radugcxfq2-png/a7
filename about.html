About

We write about the food between meals.

Why snacks got their own site

Plenty of websites will tell you how to cook dinner. Very few take the smaller moments seriously: the mid-morning gap, the box that goes to school, the tray that goes round at a birthday. Those moments add up to a large share of what a household eats in a week, and they are usually decided in a hurry.

Bitesly exists to slow that decision down just enough to make it a good one. We publish two long guides and three planners, and we keep them current rather than adding new pages for the sake of it.

How we work

Each guide is written from practice, checked against published food-safety guidance for storage times and temperatures, and read by a second person before it goes live. Quantities in the planners are rounded, sensible estimates for healthy adults and children. They are a place to start, not a prescription.

What you will find here

  • Practical guides you can follow with ordinary kitchen equipment
  • Planners that run in your browser and store nothing
  • Plain statements of what we know and what is only a rule of thumb
  • Corrections made openly when a reader spots a mistake

What you will not find

  • Medical or dietary advice for specific conditions
  • Products for sale, or paid placements dressed up as recommendations
  • Invented reviews, awards or expert endorsements
  • Promises about weight, health or results
